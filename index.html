I'm Wielotho Nyuthe, currently pursuing my B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at NIT Nagaland.

My academic journey has given me exposure to control systems, digital signal processing, power systems, embedded systems, microprocessors and microcontrollers, electronics, MATLAB/Simulink and machine-learning concepts.

I enjoy understanding how systems work rather than simply memorising the theory. Whether it is modelling a system, analysing a signal, studying an electrical network or developing a project, I like breaking a problem into smaller pieces and working through it.

This portfolio is a record of what I have learned and built so far. It is also a place to keep track of where I am going next as I continue developing as an engineer.
